Biography

Vellúa is a global music project that builds a bridge between silence and understanding, blending Middle Eastern traditions with modern sonic landscapes.

Founded by brothers Haval and Joe Haco, Vellúa creates multidimensional soundscapes designed to transcend borders. Their music features instruments and vocals from diverse traditions, combined with their own invented language, "Vellúan", which communicates directly with human emotions.

From the exotic journey of their debut album "Portrait of Souls" to collaborations with legends like Ciwan Haco and Denez Prigent, Vellúa has gained international recognition for their atmospheric and spiritually resonant compositions.
